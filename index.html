The Ninety-Sixth Year

The Kingdom marks its 96th National Day with fireworks in nine cities, a mandated visual identity and a slogan that points at the land itself. The celebration has become one of the things the state builds, and the centenary now shares a year with Vision 2030.

"...the great blessings bestowed upon this country, and for the honor of serving the Grand Mosque..."
Giving thanks at the Cabinet session he chaired in Jeddah on the eve of the Kingdom’s 96th National Day, and wishing the nation further achievements in its journey of growth and progress. "The launch of Ceer's first vehicles represents another step the Kingdom is taking in its journey to build a sustainable and prosperous industrial ecosystem..."
On the world premiere of the Exobot sedan and SUV, the first vehicles from Ceer, the marque the Public Investment Fund founded in 2022, produced at the Ceer Manufacturing Complex in the King Salman Automotive Cluster at King Abdullah Economic City. The company plans seven models over five years and projects more than SR30 billion of GDP contribution and 45 percent local content by 2034.

On the first striped hyena cub documented in the Neom Nature Reserve, born within a year of the center releasing two breeding pairs there in November 2025.
