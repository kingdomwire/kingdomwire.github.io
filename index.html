Minister of Commerce · Chairman, Ehsan Charitable Foundation board of trustees
The transformation gives Ehsan "greater powers and flexibility" to keep pace with changes in the nonprofit sector.
Two royal orders converted the Ehsan platform into an independent charitable foundation governed by a nine-member board of trustees, chaired by the commerce minister with the human resources minister as vice chair. Since its establishment in 2021, Ehsan has processed more than 440 million donations totaling about SR17 billion across social, religious, educational, food, housing and healthcare programs.

Minister of Education · New school year announcements
"I bet on teachers in the Kingdom."
Speaking at Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University days before the August 23 start of the school year, the education minister announced flexible working hours that let teachers leave when scheduled duties end, a ban on out-of-field teaching assignments, and more than 37,500 promotions for teachers and administrators due in September. Promotion opportunities have grown to more than 71,000 over two years, against roughly 7,000 in the five years before.
